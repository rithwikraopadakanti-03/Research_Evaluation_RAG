# Attendance Policy

Riverbend Institute of Technology requires every undergraduate student to maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each course. Students whose attendance falls between 65 and 74 percent may apply for condonation by paying a fee of 500 rupees per course. Students below 65 percent attendance are detained and must repeat the course in the next semester.

Medical leave requires a certificate from a registered doctor submitted within five working days of returning to class. Leave taken for official institute events, such as sports meets and technical competitions, is counted as present when approved by the head of department.

Attendance is recorded through the campus mobile app using a QR code displayed by the faculty at the start of every class. Faculty may close the QR window ten minutes after the class begins, and late students are marked absent for that session. Students can view their attendance percentage on the student portal, which updates every night at midnight.

If a student finds an error in their attendance record, they must raise a correction request on the portal within seven days. The course instructor reviews the request and the academic office makes the final decision.
