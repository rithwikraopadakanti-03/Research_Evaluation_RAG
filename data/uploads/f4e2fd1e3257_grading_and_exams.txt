Grading and Examination Rules

Each course is evaluated through continuous assessment worth 40 marks and an end semester examination worth 60 marks. Continuous assessment includes two mid semester tests of 15 marks each and ten marks for assignments and quizzes. A student must score at least 40 percent in the end semester examination and 50 percent overall to pass a course.

Grades are awarded on a ten point scale. A grade of O carries 10 points, A plus carries 9 points, A carries 8 points, B plus carries 7 points, B carries 6 points, and C carries 5 points. A grade of F means the student has failed and must appear for the supplementary examination.

The Semester Grade Point Average is the sum of credits multiplied by grade points divided by the total credits of the semester. The Cumulative Grade Point Average combines all semesters completed so far. A student needs a CGPA of at least 5.0 to be awarded the degree.

Students who fail a course may appear for the supplementary examination held four weeks after the results are published. Revaluation of an answer script can be requested within ten days of the result for a fee of 1000 rupees per paper. Revaluation results are announced within three weeks and the better of the two scores is retained.

Use of mobile phones, smart watches, or any electronic device in the examination hall is treated as malpractice. A student caught with an unauthorised device is awarded zero marks in that paper and is referred to the disciplinary committee. Students must carry their institute ID card to every examination, and entry is not permitted after thirty minutes from the start time.
