Hostel Rules and Regulations

Hostel residents must return to the hostel before 9 pm on weekdays and before 10 pm on weekends. First year students have an earlier curfew of 8:30 pm. Late entry requires written permission from the warden, and three late entries in a month lead to a warning letter to parents.

Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between 4 pm and 6 pm on Sundays. Overnight guests are not permitted under any circumstances. Parents may stay in the guest house for up to two nights with prior approval from the chief warden.

Each room is shared by two students and is fitted with a bed, a study table, a cupboard, and a ceiling fan. Electric heaters, induction stoves, and immersion rods are banned in rooms. If such an appliance is found, it is confiscated and a penalty of 2000 rupees is imposed.

Mess fees are charged monthly at 4500 rupees and cover three meals and an evening snack. A student who will be away for more than seven continuous days can apply for a mess rebate by informing the mess committee in advance. The mess serves vegetarian and non vegetarian food on alternate days, and a separate vegetarian counter runs every day.

Wi-Fi is available in all rooms with a monthly data limit of 60 gigabytes per student. Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and is punishable by suspension or expulsion, and the anti ragging helpline number is displayed on every floor.
