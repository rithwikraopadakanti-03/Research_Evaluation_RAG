# Scholarships and Financial Aid

Riverbend Institute of Technology offers three main scholarships. The Merit Excellence Scholarship waives 50 percent of the tuition fee for students who score above 90 percent in their entrance examination. The Founders Grant covers the full tuition fee for up to ten students per year whose family income is below three lakh rupees. The Sports Achievement Award gives a yearly stipend of 25000 rupees to students who have represented the state or the country.

To keep a scholarship, a student must maintain a CGPA of at least 8.0 and attendance of at least 85 percent. Scholarship holders who fall below these limits receive a warning for one semester, and the scholarship is withdrawn if they do not recover in the following semester.

Applications open on the first of July and close on the thirty first of July each year. Applicants must upload the income certificate, the previous marks card, and a bank passbook copy on the scholarship portal. Results are announced by the middle of August, and the amount is credited to the fee account before the semester begins.

Students can also apply for a zero interest education loan of up to two lakh rupees from the institute loan fund, which must be repaid within two years after graduation. Only one scholarship can be held at a time, but a student may combine a scholarship with the education loan.
