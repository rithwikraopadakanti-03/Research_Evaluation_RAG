# Library Rules

The central library at Riverbend Institute of Technology is open from 8 in the morning to 10 at night on weekdays and from 9 to 5 on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and national holidays. During examination weeks the library stays open until midnight.

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow six books for 21 days, and research scholars may borrow ten books for 30 days. Each book can be renewed twice, provided no other member has reserved it.

The overdue fine is 2 rupees per day for each book. A book lost by a student must be replaced with the same edition, or the student must pay three times the price of the book. Reference books, journals, and previous year question papers cannot be taken outside the library.

The digital library gives access to more than forty thousand e-books and journal articles, which can be reached from inside the campus network or from home through the institute VPN. Group study rooms can be booked for up to two hours at a time through the library website, and a booking is cancelled automatically if nobody arrives within fifteen minutes.

Silence must be maintained in the reading halls. Food and drinks other than water are not allowed inside the library.
